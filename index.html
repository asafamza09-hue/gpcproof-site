What is GPC?

Global Privacy Control is a browser signal (Sec-GPC: 1 header and navigator.globalPrivacyControl) that tells a website the visitor opts out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. California (CCPA/CPRA) and several other US states treat it as a valid opt-out request for businesses that may be covered by those laws.

What we test

  • A control visit and a GPC-on visit, using a clean Chromium browser from a US IP.
  • Your homepage and a product page.
  • Every network request to ad and analytics platforms (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap, Reddit, Criteo, Google, Microsoft and others).
  • Whether those requests carry user identifiers (e.g. Meta fbp/external_id, TikTok _ttp, Snap u_c1) and whether any limited-use flag is set.

What we found in a random sample

66 of 196 (≈34%)

Shopify-based stores in a random sample sent at least one ad-platform user identifier to a third party while GPC was on (95% confidence interval roughly 27–41%).

Method: 200 sites drawn at random (seed 42) from Shopify stores in the Tranco top-sites list; 196 loaded successfully. Each was visited with and without GPC; a site counts only if a real user identifier was sent with GPC on. A Trade Desk cookie alone was not counted. Google and Microsoft tags were tallied separately. This is an observation of network traffic, not a legal conclusion.
